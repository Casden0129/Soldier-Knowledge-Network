# Study Guide Standards

Every future SKN study guide must include:

- Governing publication title and identifier
- Publication date and current-status check
- Exact chapter, section, or paragraph references
- Date reviewed
- Reviewer identity or editorial role
- Clear distinction between quoted rules, plain-language explanation, and study tips
- Warning when a source is superseded or under review
- Direct link to the official publication

Questions and answers must not rely on memory alone. Operational, unit-sensitive, CUI, or test-compromise content is prohibited.
