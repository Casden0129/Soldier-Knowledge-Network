Disclaimer and Resource Classification

How to interpret SKN links, labels, and verification dates.

SKN is independent and unofficial.

The views and information presented by SKN do not necessarily represent the Department of Defense, Department of the Army, or any other government organization. External links do not constitute endorsement.

Official authority

SKN helps users locate information; it does not establish policy. Users must rely on the responsible official organization, current publication, chain of command, legal advisor, human-resources office, security manager, medical provider, or other authorized authority for decisions.

Source classifications

Official Army

A destination operated by or presented as an official Department of the Army resource.

Official DoD

A destination operated by a Department of Defense organization or enterprise service.

Official Federal

A destination operated by another United States federal organization.

Army-affiliated nonprofit

A separate nonprofit closely associated with support to the Army community. This label does not make it an Army website.

Verification date

The date shows when the SKN data record and destination were last reviewed. It does not guarantee uninterrupted availability, successful CAC authentication, or access from every network.

Access labels

CAC, Army network, VPN, DS Logon, account, and authorization labels are informational. Local security controls and system permissions may differ.

Protected marks

SKN uses an original text-based identity and does not claim official Army endorsement. Army and DoD names are used only to identify the organizations and resources being linked.
