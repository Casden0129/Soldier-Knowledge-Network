Privacy

How SKN minimizes data collection and keeps basic personalization on your device.

Current privacy posture

SKN does not require an account, email address, name, DoD ID, or profile. The public-alpha website does not intentionally collect personal records, medical information, credentials, or CUI.

Favorites and audience preference

Favorites and the selected audience filter are stored in your browser using local storage. They remain on that browser and are not transmitted to an SKN database. Clearing browser site data removes them.

External websites

Opening a resource takes you to an external website governed by that site’s privacy, security, authentication, and acceptable-use rules. Review the destination domain before opening a link.

GitHub reports

Issues submitted to the public repository can be visible to anyone. Never include credentials, PII, CUI, medical details, orders, rosters, internal screenshots, or operational information. Describe only the public navigation problem.

Future changes

If analytics, accounts, forms, or other data-processing features are proposed later, SKN should update this notice before enabling them and use the least data necessary.
