About SKN

Why the project exists, who it serves, and how it is maintained.

Mission

Make trusted Army information easier to find, understand, and use.

Who SKN serves

SKN is designed for Active Army Soldiers, Army National Guard and Army Reserve personnel, Army Civilians, contractors, family members, retirees, and veterans. Audience filters help prioritize relevant resources without creating separate websites.

What SKN is

SKN is an independent navigation and professional-development project. It links users to the organization responsible for a system or service and explains common tasks, access conditions, and ownership in plain language.

What SKN is not

SKN is not an Army system of record, identity provider, help desk, medical portal, personnel database, or official policy authority. It does not accept CAC information, passwords, personnel files, medical records, CUI, or operational data.

How information is maintained

Resources use a structured data record with an owner, destination domain, audience, access conditions, source classification, status, keywords, and review date. Public links may be checked automatically, but human review remains the standard for Army content and restricted systems.

Community corrections are welcome.

Use the Report a Problem link on a resource card or submit a structured issue through the public GitHub repository. Do not include sensitive information in a report.
